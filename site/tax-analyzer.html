About this tool

Property tax data is pulled live from the Nassau County Land Records Viewer, a public database maintained by the Nassau County Department of Assessment. Market value estimates, when available, are sourced from Zillow.

Budget context reflects the Town of Oyster Bay's adopted 2026 budget ($354M total, $242.2M tax levy), passed unanimously by the Town Board on October 21, 2025. This tool is provided by CivicSphere as a free public resource. It is not affiliated with Nassau County or the Town of Oyster Bay.
