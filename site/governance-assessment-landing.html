Sample Output

What Your Scorecard Looks Like

Every dimension is scored on a 1–5 scale based on your questionnaire responses. Your report includes not just the scores but the narrative context behind each one — what they mean for your specific organization and what to do about them.

Scores are color-coded: Green (4–5) indicates strong governance practice, Gold (3) indicates adequate but improvable, and Red (1–2) indicates a significant gap requiring prompt attention.

The overall score out of 60 gives you a single governance health benchmark you can track over time — and use in grant applications, board recruitment, and stakeholder communications.
